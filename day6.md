## ATS Resume Optimization ##

What I Did:
1.Analyzed my existing resume using AI-powered ATS optimization techniques.
2.Evaluated resume structure, formatting, keyword relevance, and ATS compatibility.
3.Reorganized the resume into a clean, ATS-friendly layout.
4.Added a professional summary aligned with Software Developer and Full-Stack Developer roles.
5.Improved project descriptions by including relevant technologies and keywords.
6.Structured technical skills into categorized sections for better ATS parsing.
7.Enhanced internship and academic information for improved recruiter visibility.
8.Compared ATS scores before and after optimization to measure improvements.


What I Learned:
1.ATS systems prioritize structure, keywords, and readability over visual design.
2.A well-formatted resume significantly improves parsing accuracy.
3.Professional summaries help communicate career goals and technical strengths effectively.
4.Keyword optimization increases the chances of passing ATS screening.
5.Clear section headings improve both ATS recognition and recruiter experience.
6.Project descriptions should highlight technologies and impact rather than just project names.
7.Technical skills should be organized logically to improve keyword matching.
8.Resume optimization is not about adding more content; it's about presenting existing information effectively.
