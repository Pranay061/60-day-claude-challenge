## Building My Personal AI Learning System with Claude ##
 
 What I Did:

• Analyzed my daily AI usage patterns and learning goals

• Compared Claude Haiku, Sonnet, and Opus models

• Identified the best model for coding, learning, research, and career preparation

• Designed a personalized AI workflow for daily productivity

• Created a visual strategy poster summarizing my findings

What I Learned:

• Different Claude models serve different purposes

• Sonnet offers the best balance for students and developers

• Effort levels significantly impact output quality and reasoning depth

• AI can be used as a learning partner, not just an answer generator

• Having a structured AI workflow improves learning efficiency and project execution
