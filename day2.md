Day 2 – Prompt Engineering Poster Challenge

What I Did Today:

Today’s challenge was to create a visually engaging poster explaining the importance of Prompt Engineering.

I designed a poster that covers:

* What Prompt Engineering is
* Why it is important when working with AI
* The difference between a weak prompt and an improved prompt
* How prompt quality affects AI-generated outputs
* Practical tips for writing better prompts

The poster uses visual comparisons, icons, and structured sections to make the concept easy to understand and appealing to viewers.


What I Learned Today:

1. Better Prompts Produce Better Results

The quality of AI responses depends heavily on how clearly and specifically instructions are written.

2. Context Matters

Providing background information helps AI understand the task more accurately and generate relevant responses.

3. Specificity Improves Accuracy

Detailed prompts reduce ambiguity and lead to more focused and actionable outputs.

4. Examples Guide AI Effectively

Including examples in prompts helps AI understand the expected format, style, and outcome.

5. Constraints Improve Responses

Specifying word count, tone, audience, or format makes outputs more aligned with requirements.

6. Iteration is Important

Prompt engineering is an iterative process. Refining prompts based on results can significantly improve output quality.
